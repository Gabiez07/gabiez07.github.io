View app demo
Initial prompt

“I want to build a web app called Planazo. It is a mobile app that helps users organize outings and activities with friends. It is designed for people who are not tech-savvy. It should have 4 tables: groups, members, plans, and votes.”

Test prompt

“I entered the app and there is an error that prevents me from going back to the home screen. Create a new back button. It should also be more colorful and informal, with a friends vibe. Use the language and themes of the local target audience, such as Argentine.”

Final result

The AI model generated an intuitive, simple, and useful app that met the instructions and proposed objective. Anyone can use it from their phone to suggest and vote on plans with friends or family.
